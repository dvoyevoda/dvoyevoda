### Internal AI Knowledge Assistant · WhyMaker

Built an LLM-powered assistant across **5GB+ of company documents**, reducing information retrieval time from **15 minutes to under 5 seconds**. The RAG pipeline supported **15+ document types** with **over 90% query accuracy** at **under $20/month**, with a streaming interface and Google OAuth SSO for company-wide access.

**Built with:** Next.js, TypeScript, OpenAI GPT-5-mini, Vertex AI Search

### qPower · Accenture

Rebuilt a non-functional battery-scheduling demo into a **live, containerized AWS deployment**. Modeled fleets of **10+ batteries** across quantum, hybrid, and classical solvers, formulated QUBO optimization problems, and presented the completed demo to **50+ Accenture professionals** worldwide.

**Built with:** React, D-Wave Ocean SDK, Docker, AWS ECS, AWS Amplify

### ProvenanceGate · CUNY Research

Developed a provenance-aware delegation system that tracks source trust across multi-agent workflows and blocks high-impact actions influenced by prompt injection. Presented the research at the **2026 CUNY Undergraduate Research Conference** and the **City Tech CRSP/CIRE Summer Symposium**.

### Visual Cryptography · CUNY Research

Built a Python/OpenCV pipeline for **lossless X-ray and MRI reconstruction**, evaluated using PSNR, MNCC, and BER. Presented the research at **2 CUNY conferences attended by 200+ participants**.

[Explore the repository →](https://github.com/dvoyevoda/Bit-Plane-Decomposition-Visual-Cryptography)

### Discussion Intelligence Toolkit · Break Through Tech AI

Developing an **open-source Python/NLP system** with a 7-person team to turn threaded online discussions into evidence-linked insight reports. The work combines discourse classification, quality scoring, embeddings, topic clustering, retrieval, and grounded LLM synthesis.
